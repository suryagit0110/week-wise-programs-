Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin) is a Japanese dark-fantasy, action, and mystery anime created by Hajime Isayama.

About Shingeki no Kyojin

The story takes place in a world where humanity lives inside enormous walls to protect itself from terrifying humanoid creatures known as Titans.

The story initially follows Eren Yeager, his adopted sister Mikasa Ackerman, and their friend Armin Arlert. After a devastating Titan attack destroys their home and changes their lives forever, Eren becomes determined to eliminate every Titan.

Main Highlights

  • Eren Yeager – A determined young soldier obsessed with achieving freedom.
  • Mikasa Ackerman – An exceptionally skilled fighter and one of Eren's closest companions.
  • Armin Arlert – A strategic thinker whose intelligence becomes extremely valuable.
  • Levi Ackerman – One of humanity's strongest soldiers and a fan-favorite character.
  • Titans – Giant humanoid creatures that threaten humanity's survival.
  • Survey Corps – The military group that ventures beyond the walls to discover the truth.
  • The Walls – Massive structures protecting humanity from Titans.
  • Mystery & Plot Twists – The anime constantly reveals new information that changes the viewer's understanding of the story.
